NotoSansSC-subset.otf: a subset of Noto Sans SC Regular 2.004 (https://github.com/notofonts/noto-cjk),
made with tools/subset_font.py --sc (ASCII, Latin-1, punctuation, CJK symbols, full-width forms and
the characters of GB 2312: the 6763 hanzi of everyday Simplified Chinese; no hinting).
