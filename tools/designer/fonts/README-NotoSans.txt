NotoSans-subset.ttf: a subset of Noto Sans Regular 2.015 (https://github.com/notofonts/latin-greek-cyrillic),
made with tools/subset_font.py --latin (Latin with Latin-1, Extended-A and -B, Greek, Cyrillic,
punctuation, currency signs, letterlike symbols; no hinting).
